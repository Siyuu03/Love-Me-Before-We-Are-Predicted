# AI-use declaration

## English

ChatGPT and OpenAI Codex were used as assistive tools during the research and development of *Love Me Before We Are Predicted*. Their roles included research support, drafting and editing prose, generating and revising code, debugging, test design, architecture review, and iterative exploration of visual parameters and interaction behaviour.

The artist defined the concept and ethical position, trained and selected the project-specific model, directed the installation and visual language, built and calibrated the physical work, reviewed AI-assisted output, and made the final decisions. Suggested code and text were accepted, changed, or rejected through human review. The artist remains responsible for testing, attribution, privacy decisions, and publication.

The Teachable Machine classifier is a separate machine-learning component in the artwork. It is intentionally presented as a limited and potentially misleading algorithmic gaze. Neither generative-AI assistance nor the classifier establishes a participant's real gender, identity, personality, consent, compatibility, or relationship.

No secret, camera device identifier, browser permission, serial authorization, or participant video is intentionally included in this repository.

## 中文

本项目在研究与开发过程中使用了 ChatGPT 和 OpenAI Codex，协助研究、文字起草与修订、代码编写与修改、调试、测试设计、架构检查，以及视觉参数和互动行为的迭代。

艺术家负责作品概念与伦理立场、项目模型的训练与选择、实体装置及视觉语言、现场搭建与校准、AI 输出审阅和最终决策，并对测试、署名、隐私与发布负责。Teachable Machine 分类器是作品中刻意受限且可能误读人的“算法凝视”，不能判断参与者的真实性别、身份、性格、同意、匹配度或关系事实。
